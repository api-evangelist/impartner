Does it still make sense to request a demo for professional software at a time when a quarter of new car buyers gladly will skip a test drive? Yes, say enterprise software experts including Bryce Maughan, director of sales enablement and business development at Impartner. “The benefits of taking a demo are many regardless of whether that demo leads to a sale or not,” Maughan says.
