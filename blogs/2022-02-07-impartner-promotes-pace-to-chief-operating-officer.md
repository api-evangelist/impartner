Impartner, the world's most complete channel management platform and Partner Relationship Management (PRM) provider, today announced the promotion of Brad Pace, currently the VP of Operations, to Chief Operating Officer. In his role, Pace will oversee general operations including customer success, customer support, implementation and project management, IT and training and documentation.
