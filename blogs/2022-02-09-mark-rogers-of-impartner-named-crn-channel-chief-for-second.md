Impartner, the global pure-play leader in SaaS-based channel management and Partner Relationship Management (PRM), today announced that CRN, a brand of The Channel Company, has named Mark Rogers, SVP of Alliances and Strategic Relationships, to its 2022 Channel Chiefs list. CRN’s annual Channel Chiefs project identifies top IT channel vendor executives who continually demonstrate expertise, influence and innovation in channel leadership.
